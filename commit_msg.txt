fix(debug): Restore mutation controls and timeline in debug panel

Three bugs fixed:

1. Premium switch was a dead toggle — the panel cast `status is BasicAdStatus`
   which always failed for custom providers. Added `setPremiumForDebug(bool)`
   to the `IAdStatusProvider` interface so the panel works with any
   implementation.

2. Missing config toggles — Global Ads Enabled, Simulate Native Failure, and
   Enable Rewarded Break were in the old DebugPanelScreen but never ported to
   MonetixDebugPanel. Added `setAdsEnabledForDebug`, `setSimulateNativeFailureForDebug`,
   and `setEnableRewardedBreakForDebug` to `IAdConfigProvider` and wired them
   into the mutation controls section.

3. Timeline was empty — the example used raw PlaygroundAnalytics instead of
   wrapping it in DiagnosticAdAnalytics, so the `is DiagnosticAdAnalytics` check
   failed. Wrapped it with DiagnosticAdAnalytics(PlaygroundAnalytics()).

Also added debug method overrides to RevenueCatAdStatusProvider and DebugAdConfig
in the example, and wired a standalone MonetixRequestCoordinator into the facade.
